## 💡 Project Overview

VERTEX is a stress and workload management system designed
to help university students understand their overall workload,
identify overload risks, and make better decisions about their
commitments.

---

## 🎯 Problem

University students often juggle:

- Assignments
- Part-time work
- Exercise
- Social commitments
- Errands
- Personal responsibilities

However, they may not realise how overloaded they actually are.

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

VERTEX helps users:

1. Track their commitments
2. Analyse their workload
3. Detect potential overload
4. Simulate "What If?" scenarios
5. Rebalance their workload
6. Receive recovery recommendations

---

## 🤖 AI Conversational Input

Instead of filling out multiple forms, users can simply tell
VERTEX what they have going on.

Example:

> "I have an assignment due next Friday and need to work
> Saturday for 6 hours."

VERTEX interprets the information and organises it into
structured workload data.
