# 1. 💡 Project Overview

## 1.1 The Problem
University students often have to manage multiple responsibilities at the same time, including academic work, part-time jobs, social commitments, personal errands, and physical activities. While each responsibility may seem manageable on its own, their combined demands can become overwhelming.

The main problem is that students often do not have a clear understanding of their overall workload or capacity. Traditional productivity tools usually focus on individual tasks, deadlines, or schedules, while wellness applications tend to focus mainly on stress or mental well-being. As a result, students may manage each responsibility separately without seeing how different commitments contribute to their overall workload.

This can lead to students overcommitting, procrastinating, prioritising tasks poorly, and failing to recognise early signs of overload. By the time they realise that they are carrying too much, their stress and workload may already have reached an unhealthy level.

The problem is therefore not simply that students have "too many tasks." It is that they lack a simple way to understand, evaluate, and rebalance their overall workload before it leads to burnout.

## 1.2 Target Users
Primary Target Users
VertexLoad is designed primarily for university and college students who manage multiple responsibilities alongside their studies. These students may have different combinations of academic, work, social, personal, and physical commitments.
Typical users may include:
-	Full-time students managing multiple assignments, projects, presentations, and deadlines. 
-	Working students balancing part-time jobs with their academic responsibilities. 
-	Active students involved in clubs, competitions, sports, or other extracurricular activities. 
-	Socially active students who frequently have events, outings, and commitments with friends or organisations. 
-	Students managing multiple personal responsibilities, such as errands, appointments, family responsibilities, and exercise. 
Typical User Scenario
A student may have several assignments due within the same week, work a part-time shift on the weekend, attend club activities, exercise regularly, and still need time for personal errands. Individually, these commitments may seem manageable, but together they can create an excessive workload.
VertexLoad is intended for students who need a clearer picture of how their different commitments affect their overall capacity, especially when they are unsure whether they can realistically take on additional responsibilities.
User Needs
The target users need a solution that can:
-	Understand their different commitments in one place. 
-	Help them recognise when their workload is becoming excessive. 
-	Identify which areas are contributing most to their overload. 
-	Help them evaluate the impact of taking on additional commitments. 
-	Suggest ways to rebalance their workload. 
-	Encourage appropriate recovery before prolonged overload leads to burnout.

## 1.3 Our Solutions
VertexLoad is a Stress & Workload Manager designed to help students understand and manage the combined demands of their daily lives. Instead of focusing only on individual tasks or stress levels, VertexLoad brings different types of commitments together and analyses how they affect the student's overall workload.
Users can interact with VertexLoad through a conversational AI assistant, allowing them to describe their commitments naturally instead of manually filling in multiple forms. For example, a student can simply say, “I have an assignment due next Friday and I need to work for six hours this weekend.” VertexLoad interprets the information, organises it into structured activities, and updates the user's workload.
The system then analyses the user's workload across multiple dimensions, including mental, time, physical, social, and personal responsibilities. It can identify potential overload, simulate the impact of additional commitments, and suggest ways to rebalance tasks and make time for recovery.
The overall concept follows:
Track → Analyse → Predict → Rebalance → Recover
Through this approach, VertexLoad aims to help students recognise overload before it develops into burnout, while giving them practical ways to make better decisions about their time and commitments.

## 1.4 Core Features
VertexLoad consists of seven core features that work together to help students understand, manage, and rebalance their overall workload.
1.	Conversational AI Input
Conversational AI is used as the primary interaction method for entering commitments. Users can describe their commitments naturally, and VertexLoad interprets the information and converts it into structured workload data for analysis.
Purpose: Reduces input friction and allows users to communicate their commitments quickly and naturally.
2.	Personal Load Dashboard
The Personal Load Dashboard provides an overview of the user's workload across different areas, including mental, time, physical, social, and personal errands.
Purpose: Helps users understand which areas are contributing most to their overall workload and identify potential sources of overwhelm.
3.	Overload Risk Score
The Overload Risk Score analyses the user's workload and available capacity to estimate their current risk of becoming overloaded.
Purpose: Alerts users when their workload is approaching or exceeding their recommended capacity, allowing them to take action before the situation becomes unmanageable.
4.	Load Balancer
The Load Balancer helps users decide which commitments should be completed, delayed, reduced, or treated as optional when their workload becomes too high.
Purpose:
Helps users actively rebalance their commitments instead of simply informing them that they are overloaded.
5.	"What If?" Simulator
The What-If Simulator allows users to simulate the impact of a new commitment on their overall workload before deciding whether to take it on.
Purpose: Helps students make informed decisions about whether they can realistically accommodate additional commitments.
6.	Recovery Recommendations
Recovery Recommendations provide suitable suggestions when the user's workload becomes high, such as taking breaks, getting sufficient sleep, exercising, or spending time away from screens.
Purpose: Helps users respond to overload and prioritise recovery instead of focusing only on productivity.
7.	Weekly Summary & Trends
Weekly Summary & Trends allows users to review changes in their workload over time and identify recurring patterns.
Purpose: Helps users recognise increasing workload trends and potential burnout patterns before they become a repeated cycle.
